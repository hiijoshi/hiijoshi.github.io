About Himanshu Joshi

I am Himanshu Joshi, a Computer Science student and full-stack developer. I work on real web projects rather than just tutorials — building admin dashboards, business platforms, deployment pipelines, and SEO-ready websites from the ground up.

My core stack includes React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Next.js on the frontend and backend side, along with deployment tools like AWS Lightsail, Nginx, PM2, and Vercel for hosting and production setup. I also spend a lot of time working with Google Search Console, sitemap and indexing fixes, and AdSense readiness — and I document these as practical case studies on my blog instead of just theory.

What I've Built

  • MandiCentral — a business operations platform with billing, ledgers, reports and PDF generation
  • A Hindi news website with a full admin panel for publishing and category management
  • Multiple production deployments using AWS Lightsail, Nginx and PM2

Why I Share This

Most tutorials skip the messy, real-world part of building software — deployment errors, indexing problems, SEO mistakes, and the small decisions that actually matter. I write about those exact problems on this site so other developers can learn from real experience instead of starting from scratch.

This website hosts my blog, project case studies, free tools for students and small businesses, and information about the services I offer.
